Meet the Band

NightHawk is truly unique, featuring multiple lead vocalists and tight harmonies.

Each member of this creative entity is both a skilled player and a gifted songwriter.

This amazing blend of talent produces phenomenal performances, distinctive melodies, awesome harmonies and a variety of songs that weave together to form the musical fabric of NightHawk.
